
\section{Question: which reconstruction errors are actually identifiable?}
A distorted dq flux map can resemble a physical parameter error without uniquely identifying one. Resistance mismatch, coherent frame rotation, current relabeling, voltage error, speed error, and inverter effects enter the same reconstructed quantities through different transformations. The central question is therefore not which curve looks most plausible, but which error directions are linearly independent on the measured support.

This paper derives those error directions before attempting estimation. Symmetry residuals are used as a compact observation channel because they remove the ideal magnetic component under the declared reflection model. The resulting sensitivity columns can then be stacked across operating points and speeds, and their rank and conditioning inspected. Synthetic examples verify the algebra and expose exact confounders. Experimental attribution is deliberately kept outside this paper unless an independent calibration closes the corresponding nullspace.
