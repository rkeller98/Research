\section{Conclusion}
Reconstruction errors act through translations, rotations, argument changes
and speed-dependent scaling. Their conditional sensitivity columns define
which combinations are observable. Synthetic tests verify these transformations
and recovery under a known reference; the portable measured support confirms
an exact resistance--voltage nuisance nullspace that extra speeds cannot remove.
Independent calibration and state information must close such directions
before the residual geometry can justify a physical error correction.
