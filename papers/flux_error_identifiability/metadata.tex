\title{Geometric Fingerprints and Identifiability of dq Flux Reconstruction Errors}
\author{}
\date{7 October 2026}
\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{Which measurement and parameter errors can be distinguished from reconstructed dq flux? Exact and first-order transformations expose resistance mismatch, coherent rotor-frame rotation, current relabeling, voltage and speed errors. A stacked sensitivity operator separates local rank from conditioning and reveals an exact resistance versus current-proportional voltage confounder. Synthetic tests verify signs, coordinate chain rules and recovery under qualified assumptions. Evaluating the confounding columns on portable measured operating-point support confirms that additional speeds do not remove an identical nuisance direction. No physical sensor error is estimated from that support test.}
