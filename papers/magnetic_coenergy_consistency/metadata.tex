\title{Integrability Tests for Measured dq Magnetic Flux Fields}
\author{}
\date{7 October 2026}
\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{Can a measured dq flux field be the gradient of one magnetic co-energy potential? Reciprocity and closed-loop circulation provide local and global tests, while reflection symmetry is a separate restriction. We derive these tests and their coordinate dependence, then evaluate portable PSM measurements using a piecewise-affine field. Exact segment integration is checked against independently clipped triangle curl integrals. Nonzero path disagreement persists without quadrature error, whereas local derivative metrics are strongly sensitive to triangle geometry. The result concerns the reconstructed terminal-current field; it neither identifies the cause nor disproves conservative storage in other conjugate coordinates.}
