\section{Question and contribution}
Can the two measured flux components be slopes of one scalar landscape?
A local mismatch of mixed derivatives and an accumulated closed-loop integral
are complementary answers. The central contribution is a consistency audit
that separates exact integration of a chosen interpolant from uncertainty in
the measured field and its interpolation.

Energy-based motor descriptions connect storage, construction symmetry and
reciprocity \cite{jebai2014,haus1989}; nonlinear flux models use coupled
differential inductance rather than independent constants \cite{sun2020}.
We test admissibility in explicitly declared coordinates before fitting a
potential. The fitting problem is a separate companion \cite{companion-reconstruction}.
The real demonstration uses one portable PSM lineage at two temperature
references. Controlled affine checks and independent Green integration verify
the numerical operation, not the physical truth of the voltage-derived flux.
