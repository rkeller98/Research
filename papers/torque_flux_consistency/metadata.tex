\title{Torque Observability of dq Flux Maps and the Remaining Radial Ambiguity}
\author{}
\date{7 October 2026}
\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{Which dq flux component is observable from an independent torque measurement? At nonzero stator current torque is a rank-one projection with a radial nullspace. We derive the solution line, its minimum-change representative and weighted variants, and show that smooth reciprocal radial potential terms remain invisible even under reflection symmetry. Synthetic checks verify observability, noise amplification and joint electrical-mechanical ranks. A portable six-phase PSM dataset is evaluated with its recorded two-system configuration: the total torque factor is 3p rather than 3p/2. This resolves the leading factor-two discrepancy without a sensor-gain fit, while calibration and shaft-loss qualifications remain open.}
