\title{Physics-Constrained Reconstruction of Saturated \texorpdfstring{$dq$}{dq} Flux-Linkage Maps from Magnetic Co-Energy}
\author{}
\date{6 October 2026}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
Stationary electrical measurements provide flux-linkage samples, not measured
magnetic energy. This learning paper develops magnetic co-energy as a latent
scalar potential whose gradient represents those samples. Reflection symmetry
enforces direct-axis even and quadrature-axis odd flux, while a common
potential enforces reciprocal differential cross-inductances. Its Hessian
describes saturation and cross-saturation as changing magnetic curvature.
Path integration explains the representation and its gauge freedom, but
direct regularized gradient fitting avoids constructing path-dependent noisy
energy samples. A symmetry-constrained tensor-product B-spline potential is
developed as one numerical candidate and compared with independent flux fits
in a small reproducible synthetic study. Flux accuracy, derivative quality,
parity, integrability, and the influence of sparse data and outliers are assessed
separately. Physical constraints characterize admissibility within an assumed
quasi-static conservative model; they do not guarantee recovery of the true
machine from biased measurements. A companion paper interprets structured
departures from this admissible class as diagnostic information.}
