\title{Symmetry-Based Identification and Correction of Stator-Resistance and Rotor-Angle Errors from \texorpdfstring{$dq$}{dq} Flux-Linkage Maps}
\author{}
\date{6 October 2026}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
Flux reconstruction, magnetic symmetry, and reference-frame alignment answer
different questions. This learning paper derives their connection without
assuming constant inductances. An even magnetic co-energy in quadrature
current implies an even direct-axis flux and an odd quadrature-axis flux,
including saturation and cross-saturation. A resistance mismatch adds a
speed-scaled transverse current term. A rotor-angle offset rotates both the
current argument and the resulting flux; its first-order sensitivity is
the difference between a differential-inductance term and a flux-rotation
term. Forbidden parity channels provide two diagnostic residuals, whose
stacked sensitivities determine local identifiability and conditioning.
Reproducible synthetic examples compare exact rotations with linearization,
check proposed corrections, and expose voltage-error confounding. A companion
co-energy reconstruction framework defines the admissible magnetic class;
the present paper interprets its complementary residual structure. The results
support proposed calibration corrections under explicit quasi-static,
thermal, and measurement assumptions; they are not experimental validation.}
