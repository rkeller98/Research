\title{On the Interpretation of Symmetry Residuals in Experimental \texorpdfstring{$dq$}{dq} Flux-Linkage Maps}
\author{}
\date{6 October 2026}

\newcommand{\PaperAbstract}{%
Forbidden reflection components of experimentally reconstructed flux-linkage
maps contain information that conventional symmetric averaging discards.
This paper interprets their spatial, thermal-reference, and speed dependence
without assuming constant inductances. The d-odd residual is expressed as a
resistance-equivalent quantity that equals used-minus-true stator resistance
only if a constant resistance mismatch is the sole source. Two supplied
test-bench datasets are evaluated using their existing exact-key pairing
method, without smoothing or interpolation of missing partners. At 2000 rpm,
the high-q-current medians are 0.096, -0.579 and -1.268 milliohm at rotor
references of 40, 60 and 80 degrees Celsius; the wider current region is
strongly nonconstant. At a 70-degree rotor reference, 1000 and 3000 rpm give
-2.157 and -5.334 milliohm from only six and seven high-current pairs.
A direct inverse-speed comparison on 36 common valid pairs also deviates
from the constant-resistance prediction. Exact frame rotation preserves
reciprocity while rotating the reflection axis; saturation changes its
angle sensitivity. Synthetic checks retain these analytical results and
demonstrate exact confounding by current-proportional voltage error.
The measured patterns require additional effects but do not uniquely
identify winding resistance, iron loss, angle error or inverter contributions.}
