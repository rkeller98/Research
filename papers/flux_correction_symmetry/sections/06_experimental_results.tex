The observed forbidden components are on the scale of a few mWb. Their thermal
reference dependence is descriptive: a rotor reference does not establish
winding equilibrium or identify an ohmic cause. Some mirror arguments lie
outside the measured hull and are excluded instead of extrapolated.

At each retained pair the projected values satisfy parity to roundoff.
That is a check of the operator, not experimental proof of a corrected machine.
An allowed-parity perturbation survives unchanged, however large it is.

The displayed selected region requires positive $i_q$ above 10\% of the slice
maximum and has 142/144 supported pairs at 30/70 $^\circ$C. Repeating the same
calculation over every positive-q candidate gives 156 supported pairs in each
slice. Full-map $(r_d,r_q)$ RMS is $(2.763,2.203)$ mWb at 30 $^\circ$C and
$(2.399,2.144)$ mWb at 70 $^\circ$C, compared with selected-region values
$(2.873,2.184)$ and $(2.477,2.127)$ mWb. The qualitative conclusion is not
created by excluding the near-axis region, while normalization by $i_q$ would
still be ill-conditioned there.
